# Chess Prodigy

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Chess learners play a custom coach on phones and desktops. They need time to follow
each reply, understand the ideas behind their own moves, and revisit explanations
without interrupting the game. Guests can play offline; accounts retain private progress.

## Personality and principles
Patient, clear, historically grounded. Explain chess vocabulary through the position.
Keep the board usable while reading. Preserve previous lessons throughout the game.
Separate a detected pattern from proof that a move is good. Attribute history carefully
and link further reading. Existing dark and wooden themes remain familiar.

## Anti-references
Avoid instant mechanical replies, disappearing tips, intrusive reading dialogs and
green accents, following the owner's September 2026 feedback.

## Accessibility
Maintain keyboard play, readable contrast in both themes, reduced-motion support and
comfortable phone scrolling. Automated accessibility checks complement real phone use.
